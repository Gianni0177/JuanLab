    Key Mechanics
    Suppression Field: Energy Cycler
    When combat begins, the Battle-Hardened Pioneer Secret Source Control Array will deploy 4 detection devices protected by Hydro Wards, constructing a special Suppression Field that will create a Force Shield for the Nemesis and increase all its RES except for Cryo and Electro RES. When the field has been active for some time, the Nemesis will unleash Alpha Strike, dealing AoE DMG over a massive area and retrieving all detection devices. It will then deploy a Hydro Ward whose absorption is based on the number of devices retrieved.
    Force Shield
    Due to the Ley Line disturbance, the Nemesis' Suppression Field will fail when the Force Shield is broken, causing the Nemesis to take one instance of massive rebound DMG and enter a state of paralysis.\nAdditionally, each time the Nemesis takes Superconduct and Stellar-Conduct Reaction DMG, it will lose a fixed amount of Force Shield. When this Shield is active, the amounts of Stellar-Conduct Reaction, Cryo, and Electro DMG it absorbs will further increase the rebound DMG the Nemesis will take when the Shield is broken.
    Detection Device: Pressurized Component
    Detection devices will be destroyed when their Hydro Ward is broken or if they are Frozen and then affected by Shatter, Melt, or other Elemental Reaction DMG. Due to the Ley Line disturbance, the Nemesis' Shield will be damaged for every detection device that is destroyed.

    Key Mechanics
    Deepdark Shield: Deeproot Blight
    Affected by the Ley Line disturbance, the Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares gathers Abyssal energy to create an extremely durable protective Shield. Lunar Reaction DMG is more effective at destroying this Shield. When the Shield is destroyed, the Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares takes a single instance of immense DMG and temporarily enters a weakened state.
    Rift Assault
    Affected by the Ley Line disturbance, the Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares will enter a lengthy charging state and summon multiple Fishers of Hidden Depths. Once it has fully charged, it will deal DMG over a large AoE and devour all Fishers. Defeat Fishers before this happens to destroy the Shield and interrupt the Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares' charging.

    Nightsoul's Blessing Characters
    Key Mechanics
    Adaptive Countermeasures: Cold Start
    When the battle begins, the Nemesis will gain much higher All Elemental and Physical RES. When a character enters and maintains the Nightsoul's Blessing state, the Nemesis will accumulate progress toward Adaptive Countermeasures. Due to the Ley Line disorder, after the Nemesis finishes accumulating Adaptive Countermeasures for the first time, it will lose all of the RES it gained at the start of the battle.
    Boltsphere Cannon: High-Pressure Pumps
    After Adaptive Countermeasures progress fills up, the Nemesis will activate Accretion Mechanisms and excavate 2 blocks of solid Phlogiston before absorbing energy from within them to charge the Boltsphere Cannon. The Accretion Mechanisms will generate force fields to protect themselves. You must climb up solid Phlogiston to get inside the force fields to destroy them. After all the Accretion Mechanisms are destroyed, Boltsphere Cannon charging will be interrupted, while the Nemesis will get paralyzed and simultaneously take massive rebound DMG. Due to the Ley Line disorder, Adaptive Countermeasures will accrue progress as time passes, but the threshold required to activate Boltsphere Cannon will be higher.
